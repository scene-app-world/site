Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 4, 2026

Scene ("we", "us", "our") operates the Scene mobile application. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and password (or Apple ID credentials if you use Sign in with Apple). We also store your chosen display name, username, and profile photo.

Preferences. During onboarding you select cities and interest categories (e.g., music genres, event types). These are stored to personalize your event feed.

Instagram data. When you follow an Instagram account through Scene, we retrieve that account's publicly available posts, captions, and profile information through a third-party scraping service. Post images are temporarily downloaded, analyzed by an AI model to extract event details, and then discarded. We do not permanently store Instagram images unless they are directly associated with an extracted event listing.

Event data you submit. When you share a post or image to Scene via the iOS share extension, we receive the shared image or URL, extract event details using AI, and store the resulting event information.

Device information. We collect your device model and OS version solely for push notification delivery. We do not collect unique device identifiers (IDFA, UDID, or advertising identifiers).

Usage data. We record actions you take within the app, including events you save, like, or mark as "going to," accounts you follow, and users you follow. Likes, follows, and RSVPs are visible to other Scene users.

Optional integrations. If you connect Spotify, we access your followed and top artists to suggest relevant accounts. If you connect Google Calendar, we create calendar events on your behalf. You can disconnect these integrations at any time and we will delete the associated tokens.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, personalize, and improve the Scene service
  • To extract event details from Instagram posts using AI
  • To send push notifications about new events from accounts you follow
  • To process subscriptions and manage your account
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs via error reporting

3. AI and Automated Processing

We use OpenAI's Vision API to analyze Instagram post images and captions in order to extract structured event information (event name, date, time, venue, address). Post captions and images are sent to OpenAI for this purpose. We do not use your personal information or account data for AI model training.

4. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • OpenAI — event extraction from post images and captions
  • Apify — Instagram data retrieval
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting (receives your user ID and email for error context)
  • Expo — push notification delivery
  • Spotify — artist data (only if you connect your account)
  • Google — calendar integration (only if you connect your account)

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and profile data for as long as your account is active. Extracted event data is retained indefinitely to build a useful event history. Post images associated with past events are automatically deleted after the event date passes. Push notification tokens are removed when you unregister a device or delete your account.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab in the app. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, follows, saved events, likes, RSVPs, activity history, push tokens, and all connected integration tokens. Event listings that were extracted from public posts may remain in the system as they are not personal data.

7. Location Data

Scene does not request access to your device's GPS or location services. The cities you select during onboarding are entered manually and are used only to filter events in your feed.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted on-device secure storage (iOS Keychain). Passwords are hashed and managed by Supabase Auth. We do not store passwords in plaintext.

9. Children's Privacy

Scene is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or by email. Your continued use of Scene after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
